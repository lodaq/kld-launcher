# Long-press opens App options; Dock sorted in Settings

> **Status:** Superseded. Drag-and-drop has been removed. Long-pressing an App (in the App list or the Dock) now opens an App options screen with app shortcuts (`LauncherApps.getShortcuts`), Pin/Unpin to dock, Move left/right (for Docked apps), App info, and Uninstall. Long-pressing the Settings button (or tapping **Sort dock** in Settings) opens the Dock sorting screen.

## Original Decision (Historical)

Moving apps between the App list and the Dock previously used long-press drag-and-drop, with App info moved to a "Manage apps" row in Settings.
